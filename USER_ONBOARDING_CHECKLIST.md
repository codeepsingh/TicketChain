## Onboarding Instructions for Users

**Step 1:** Visit https://ticketchain1.netlify.app/  
**Step 2:** Install Freighter wallet extension from https://www.freighter.app/  
**Step 3:** Create a Testnet account and fund it at https://laboratory.stellar.org/#account-creator?network=testnet  
**Step 4:** Connect wallet via "Connect Wallet" button in the top navigation  
**Step 5:** Perform at least one of: Browse events, Create event, Purchase ticket, Transfer ticket, Verify ticket  
**Step 6:** Note down your wallet address and share transaction hash + feedback
